# v1.4.0 — Privacy audit and a much deeper look inside

Phone Diagnostic Tool **1.4.0** (versionCode **36**). Installs over 1.3.2 in
place — no uninstall needed. No new permissions.

## Privacy

- **Privacy audit** (More → Privacy audit) — which of your apps can use the
  camera, microphone, location, contacts, SMS, call log and more. Apps with
  accessibility, notification-reading or device-admin access are highlighted,
  since that is how stalkerware operates. Tap any app to open its system
  settings and revoke access. A **This app** card lists every permission this
  tool holds and what it is for.
- **Reports leave out device identifiers by default** — build fingerprint,
  bootloader, baseband and build host. Turn it off in Settings if you need them.
- **Choose where the latency check connects** — Cloudflare (new default),
  Quad9, Google, or **your router only**, which never leaves your network.
- **Copied reports are marked sensitive** and cleared from the clipboard after
  a minute.
- **Block screenshots** — optional setting that also hides the app in Recents.

## Deeper diagnostics

- **CPU** — real cluster layout from each cluster's peak clock (e.g.
  "3 + 4 + 1 cores · 2.0 / 2.8 / 3.2 GHz"), governors and available frequencies
  for every cluster.
- **Battery** — what your charger offers in watts, drain rate and estimated
  time left from recent history. Capacity health and cycle count are now in
  reports too.
- **Storage** — flash type (UFS / eMMC) and how worn it is, where readable.
- **System activity** — how long the phone has been awake vs in deep sleep,
  what last woke it, and what kept it awake longest.
- **Thermals** — Android's own thermal status and a 10-second throttling
  forecast. The **load test** now says how much speed it kept at the end and
  flags throttled runs.
- **Media codecs** (new screen) — every video and audio format, whether
  playback and recording use hardware or software, and the maximum resolution
  and frame rate.
- **Display** — every resolution and refresh-rate mode, HDR peak brightness.
- **Network** — Wi-Fi standard (Wi-Fi 4 to 7, including 6E) and cellular
  generation (2G to 5G) with signal strength, read without any phone or
  location permission.
- **Security** — security patch age in days, StrongBox and hardware key
  storage.
- **Memory, sensors, cameras** — kernel memory stats (cached, buffers, swap,
  ZRAM), sensor reporting mode and batching, camera ISO / exposure ranges,
  flash and capabilities.

Some readings — flash wear, wake sources — are blocked for normal apps on most
phones and appear only with Shizuku or root enabled in Settings.

## Polish

- Screens remember where you were scrolled when you back out of a submenu.
- The More tab is far more compact: grouped links and one export card.
